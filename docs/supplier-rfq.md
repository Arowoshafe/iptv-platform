# Wholesale content supplier request for proposal

Subject: Licensed U.S. + Nigeria OTT/TV wholesale distribution inquiry — custom branded apps

We are developing a direct-to-consumer television subscription service with proprietary web, smart TV and mobile apps. Please provide a formal quotation and documentation addressing:

1. Which named channels and VOD titles are cleared for **US customers** and **Nigeria customers** separately, including diaspora rights where applicable?
2. Does the agreement explicitly authorize retail subscription resale to individuals, *through our proprietary branded apps*, rather than only an ISP, hotel or MDU?
3. Do you supply HLS/DASH feeds or a documented playback-authentication API? Is SRT ingest offered? What DRM is supported (Widevine, FairPlay, PlayReady)?
4. What subscriber-entitlement/provisioning API, sandbox access and webhook events are provided? Are API usage and custom app integrations included in the contract?
5. Can we receive EPG via XMLTV or JSON, plus channel artwork and metadata, with rights to display it in our app?
6. Which devices are authorized: web, Android/Google TV, Fire TV (Fire OS and Vega OS), iOS, Android mobile, Apple TV and Roku?
7. What are the one-time setup fees, minimum monthly guarantees, per-active-subscriber rates, content-tier fees, CDN charges, DRM fees and penalties?
8. What are restrictions on geographic availability, simultaneous streams, cloud hosting, catch-up, time-shift, recording, downloads and ads?
9. Who is the licensor for each channel, and can you provide verifiable evidence of upstream rights or warranties and indemnities for the territories offered?
10. What are the contract term, trial terms, termination rights, data-export rights, SLA, support coverage, outages and subscriber-migration provisions?
11. Do you have current reference implementations serving these countries and a production test feed that is contractually approved for our test environment?
12. Is our corporate structure (US company, Nigerian company or both) eligible for your agreements?

Please quote separate US and NG catalog and minimum commitment scenarios for 100, 500 and 2,000 subscribers. We will not purchase before rights verification and regulatory review.
